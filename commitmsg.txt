feat(FAN-2532): rebalance the engineer weapon-ultimate trio into its power corridor

The live FAN-2516 instrument showed engineer_sentry_wrench at 1.9% and
engineer_pressure_mines at 23% of their own corridor floor. Same root cause as the
Chemist trio in FAN-2527: all three executors price damage as context.damage *
damage, i.e. off the equipped weapon's PER-HIT channel, which inside the Engineer
spans 2.50 / 10.39 / 38.45 while the three power budgets are nearly identical
because they are priced off reference_solo_dps (~44..45 for all three). The wrench
and the drone carry their real DPS in turret and orbit-contact summon channels an
ultimate never reads; the coefficients predate the instrument and assumed a
comparable anchor.

- engineer_sentry_wrench: damage 0.55 -> 62.0. Solo effect 16.98 -> 1243.69
  (0.787 of budget), boss cap ratio 0.007 -> 0.783. It is the only cast in the
  class with no per-target cap, so it stays the one that can spend its whole
  budget on a single target.
- engineer_pressure_mines: damage 1.2 -> 8.0. Solo effect 200.56 -> 1025.64.
  A single covered target would take 1230.4, but the field's declared
  target_cap_fraction 0.65 bounds it at 1009.65 — solo and elite read the cap,
  not the coefficient, which is the anti-one-shot identity of a mine field.
  The coefficient is readable where the cap does not bind: boss 0.119 -> 0.792
  and 10293.56 applied across 17 of 20 probes.
- engineer_repair_drone: coefficients unchanged. Its declared repair channel
  measured exactly 0.00 because repair_targets() read host.player — the Player
  adapter's own field, not one of the ten contract methods — so a host that
  stands in for the hero silently lost the whole channel instead of failing
  closed. It now falls back to the host, and ultimate_host_repair() still owns
  eligibility. Healing 0.00 -> 41.79. Its 1.085 ceiling overshoot is documented
  as an explicit exception: 1560 of its 1706 solo effect is knockback impulse the
  instrument counts in pixels, while its HP-denominated save is 93.71 against a
  reference EHP of 83.57 — the decisive defensive save its control_save archetype
  asks for, and the channel neither sibling has.

No beat, formation, radius, count or interval moved, so the three downstream
visual-animation cards inherit the timing contract unchanged. The other 48 rows
are bit-identical and regressions() stays clean, so the committed baseline is not
rewritten; two runs of the report are bit-identical.

Adds tests/ultimates/engineer_balance_test.gd, the closed-form twin of the live
instrument (1243.69 / 1706.21 / 1025.65 exactly), which rebuilds the hex and the
seeded mine field with the shipped pattern_points primitive rather than a private
copy, asserts the corridor, the bounded drone exception, the per-target cap and
the three non-duplicated niches, and goes red for a runaway coefficient.
