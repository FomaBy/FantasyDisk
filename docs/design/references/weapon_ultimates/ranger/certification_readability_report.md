# Ranger weapon-ultimate certification readability report (FAN-3941)

Independent-review evidence for the Ranger ultimate trio (`ranger/moon_crossbow`, `ranger/storm_longbow`, `ranger/hunter_trap`) in the four presentation modes at the four supported viewports, regenerated after the second review because the accessibility modes now run through the production policy and the enemy-bolt probe is enforced. Every frame is a real cast started through UltimatePlayerHost on the shipped Player (its own current Camera2D, real HP, a full ultimate charge), against real Enemy scenes, a real hazard telegraph and a real enemy projectile, with the live ultimate HUD adapter, under a fixed 60 fps clock and the exclusive process admission of `tools/godot_gate.py`; the reduced-motion and photosensitivity-safe modes are the persisted production preferences published on the tree root through `scripts/settings/ultimate_accessibility_settings.gd` and honoured by the class scene driver itself. The frames are the evidence; the capture manifest pins every file hash, the source commit and tree read from git at capture time, the engine, the commands, the per-frame camera/world state, the accessibility snapshot, the measured coverage, the full-screen surface alpha and the per-run camera and surface traces.

- Engine: Godot 4.7-stable (official) (gl_compatibility, opengl3, Apple M4 Pro), macOS 26.5.1, display server macOS, captured 2026-09-09 10:27:50 UTC, fixed frame clock 60 fps.

- Capture command: `FSD_GODOT_EXCLUSIVE=1 python3 tools/godot_gate.py --path . --fixed-fps 60 --disable-vsync --script res://tests/ultimates/presentation/ranger_certification_live_capture.gd`
- Gate command: `python3 tools/godot_gate.py --headless --path . --fixed-fps 60 --script res://tests/ultimates/presentation/ranger_certification_capture_test.gd`
- Seed: 3941 (seed() is reset to this value before every cast; the shake device draws randf_range for its offsets, so the seed plus the fixed frame clock make every offset and every frame reproducible, and runs that differ only in a device stay pixel-comparable.)
- Exclusive process admission during the committed run: yes. Every certification cast ran under the explicit exclusive process admission of `tools/godot_gate.py` (`FSD_GODOT_EXCLUSIVE=1`, recorded as `capture.exclusive_gate: true`) on the fixed 60 fps clock; the renderer refuses to run without it, and refuses a dirty worktree, so the recorded source commit is the exact source the frames were rendered from.
- Real runtime: Player `res://scenes/Player.tscn`, enemies `res://scenes/Enemy.tscn`, hazards scripts/hazard_vfx.gd telegraph(radius 96) at Player offset (-190, 140) and scenes/EnemyProjectile.tscn at Player offset (-120, -180), HUD scripts/ui/ultimate_hud/ultimate_hud_runtime_adapter.gd mount() on the Player (real UltimateHudWidget, live charge/active state) plus an HP readout bound to Player.health/max_health; combat camera zoom 1.12 on the logical 2560x1440 canvas (canvas_items).
- Method: One real cast per weapon x mode x viewport in a SubViewport of the exact viewport size: the shipped Player (configure_character, full ultimate charge, its own Camera2D made current at combat zoom 1.12 x viewport_height/1440, smoothing off) at the origin; real Enemy scenes on a deterministic spiral around the aim centre (frozen in place, real HP, real hit feedback); a real HazardVfx telegraph and a real EnemyProjectile; the live UltimateHudRuntimeAdapter mounting the real UltimateHudWidget plus an HP readout bound to the Player; the cast started with UltimatePlayerHost.activate so the executor, authored scene, victim impacts and weight devices run as in the game. The engine runs under --fixed-fps 60: beat = frame index. Each frame samples the Player camera offset (reduced-motion evidence). The hero's basic weapon is held (its _process is off) so only the ultimate acts. At a beat the composite frame is read and the tree is paused; every CanvasItem outside the authored scene's subtree is hidden and the viewport rendered transparent for the authored-scene opaque-coverage measurement (alpha >= 0.5, stride 2; bounding box against the HUD band), then restored, the caption drawn and the committed frame read while still paused, so the PNG, the camera record and the coverage describe the same process step; the tree resumes afterwards. reduced_motion and photosensitivity_safe publish the persisted ultimate_accessibility_settings snapshot on the tree root (scripts/settings/ultimate_accessibility_settings.gd apply_snapshot, as main.gd does at startup) and the live cast's own driver honours it; the capture runner never touches the scene. The full-screen surface alpha of the live scene is sampled every frame. crowded stands the declared crowd cap of enemies. Readability probes project the recorded world positions through the recorded camera state.

The arena-wide BackdropVeil is a translucent tint that rises exactly once per cast, never reaches the opaque threshold and is layered at absolute z 2 (above the arena floor and the actors, below every enemy hazard telegraph at z 6-9 and enemy projectile at z 12), so it is excluded from coverage and never dims a hazard. Readability probes project the recorded world positions through the recorded camera: HUD band contrast, the Player and every enemy against the floor, the hazard telegraph's warm rim along four spokes, and the enemy bolt's drawn pixels (the texture's opaque texels) against the mean of the ring just outside it, judged at the unchanged 0.06 floor. ## Observed readability at the beats

### `ranger/moon_crossbow`

Beats sampled (presentation clock, frame): release 0.88 s (frame 53), impact 1.2 s (frame 72), active 1.83 s (frame 110), recovery 2.85 s (frame 171); declared timing {"windup": 0.0, "release": 0.7, "active": 1.05, "recovery": 2.6, "cancel": 3.1}.

- **impact** — 1.20 s: the first impact lands with the 95 ms hitstop and the camera shake (offset -2.8, 2.2 px at 648p, up to 4.9 px in the window); the four split bolts fan over the goblins with hit bursts and damage numbers; bolt 0.16, hazard 0.32.

## Limitations

- The world is a certification arena, not a spawned run: the shipped Player stands at the arena centre with its basic weapon held (its auto-attack VFX expects the game's Main scene as parent), the real Enemy scenes are frozen in place with 100000 HP so the composition depends only on the cast, and the arena floor is a flat bench colour rather than field art. Everything that moves or reads — Player, camera, enemies, hit feedback, damage numbers, HP bars, hazard telegraph, enemy projectile, HUD widget, executor, authored scene, victim impacts, weight devices — is the shipped runtime.
- The enemy bolt is a 17 px sprite at 1152x648 whose own luma sits about 0.07 above the floor, so its per-pixel contrast share is inherently low (0.14-0.18 on a bare floor); the single-shot flash of storm_longbow brings it to 0.078 for the release beat. Both are above the unchanged 0.06 floor and the flash is over by the impact beat. The earlier candidate's readings below the floor came from a probe that read the committed frame one simulation step after its camera record (fixed in this candidate) and from a padded bounding box; the probe now reads the bolt's drawn texels against their local surround.
- Ranger's executors outlive their presentations (4.45-5.35 s lifetimes against 3.1-3.8 s timelines), so every Ranger frame shows a live presentation; the hero's camera shake is the cast's only reduced-motion device and the combat director's hit shake is outside this harness.
