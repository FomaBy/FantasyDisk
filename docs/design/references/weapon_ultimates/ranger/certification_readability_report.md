# Ranger weapon-ultimate certification readability report (FAN-3941)

Independent-review evidence for the Ranger ultimate trio (`ranger/moon_crossbow`, `ranger/storm_longbow`, `ranger/hunter_trap`) in the four presentation modes at the four supported viewports, taken from the real runtime after the first independent review rejected bench stand-ins: every frame is a real cast started through UltimatePlayerHost on the shipped Player (its own current Camera2D, real HP, a full ultimate charge), against real Enemy scenes, a real hazard telegraph and a real enemy projectile, with the live ultimate HUD adapter, under a fixed 60 fps clock. The frames are the evidence; this report records what they show and what they do not.

## Provenance

- Source ref/commit/tree the shipped runtime was run from: `dev` / `d192be10bbe52dd89971cab0acc66eb92ccab37f` / `e4a423855ffab4e8c83a2e5255fef4e65f4cf5cf`. Integrated origin/dev revision whose shipped Ranger scenes, executors, Player, Enemy, hazard and HUD were run; the capture tooling, frames and this manifest are added by FAN-3941 on top of it. The Soldier grenade executor and scene driver carry FAN-3941's presentation-only payoff instrumentation.
- Engine: Godot 4.7-stable (official) (gl_compatibility, opengl3, Apple M4 Pro), macOS 26.5.1, display server macOS, captured 2026-09-09 08:36:53 UTC, fixed frame clock 60 fps.
- Renderer: `tests/ultimates/presentation/ranger_certification_live_capture.gd`; gate: `tests/ultimates/presentation/ranger_certification_capture_test.gd`; capture manifest: `docs/design/references/weapon_ultimates/ranger/certification_capture_manifest.json` (every frame's path, size, byte count, sha256, camera state, world positions, probes and measurements; every run's camera-offset trace).
- Capture command: `python3 tools/godot_gate.py --path . --fixed-fps 60 --disable-vsync --script res://tests/ultimates/presentation/ranger_certification_live_capture.gd`
- Gate command: `python3 tools/godot_gate.py --headless --path . --fixed-fps 60 --script res://tests/ultimates/presentation/ranger_certification_capture_test.gd`
- Seed: 3941 (seed() is reset to this value before every cast; the shake device draws randf_range for its offsets, so the seed plus the fixed frame clock make every offset and every frame reproducible, and runs that differ only in a device stay pixel-comparable.)
- Exclusive process admission during the committed run: no. Exclusive admission (`FSD_GODOT_EXCLUSIVE=1`) is not used for these captures: the engine runs on a fixed 60 fps clock (`--fixed-fps 60`), so every frame is a frame index of a deterministic cast and host load cannot change a pixel; the run honours the shared slot admission of `tools/godot_gate.py`.
- Real runtime: Player `res://scenes/Player.tscn`, enemies `res://scenes/Enemy.tscn`, hazards scripts/hazard_vfx.gd telegraph(radius 96) at Player offset (-190, 140) and scenes/EnemyProjectile.tscn at Player offset (-120, -180), HUD scripts/ui/ultimate_hud/ultimate_hud_runtime_adapter.gd mount() on the Player (real UltimateHudWidget, live charge/active state) plus an HP readout bound to Player.health/max_health; combat camera zoom 1.12 on the logical 2560x1440 canvas (canvas_items).
- Method: One real cast per weapon x mode x viewport in a SubViewport of the exact viewport size: the shipped Player (configure_character, full ultimate charge, its own Camera2D made current at combat zoom 1.12 x viewport_height/1440, smoothing off) at the origin; real Enemy scenes on a deterministic spiral around the aim centre (frozen in place, real HP, real hit feedback); a real HazardVfx telegraph and a real EnemyProjectile; the live UltimateHudRuntimeAdapter mounting the real UltimateHudWidget plus an HP readout bound to the Player; the cast started with UltimatePlayerHost.activate so the executor, authored scene, victim impacts and weight devices run as in the game. The engine runs under --fixed-fps 60: beat = frame index. Each frame samples the Player camera offset (reduced-motion evidence). The hero's basic weapon is held (its _process is off) so only the ultimate acts. At a beat the composite frame is read, then the tree is paused, every CanvasItem outside the authored scene's subtree is hidden and the viewport rendered transparent for the authored-scene opaque-coverage measurement (alpha >= 0.5, stride 2; bounding box against the HUD band), then restored. reduced_motion sets the shipped screen_shake toggle off on the tree root; photosensitivity_safe additionally zeroes the modulate of every node the live scene flags as fullscreen_layer; crowded stands the declared crowd cap of enemies. Readability probes project the recorded world positions through the recorded camera state.

## Coverage

- Weapon x mode x viewport combinations: 48; one real cast per combination (48 runs), each combination committed at the active beat and every payoff beat, plus release/impact/recovery at 1152x648.
- Frames committed: 84 of 84 expected (release/active/recovery at the midpoint of the declared phase window, impact 0.15 s after the active edge; the active beat and every payoff beat are committed at every viewport, the others at 648p).
- Viewports: `648p` 1152x648 (camera zoom 0.504, UI scale 0.45), `720p` 1280x720 (camera zoom 0.56, UI scale 0.5), `1080p` 1920x1080 (camera zoom 0.84, UI scale 0.75), `2k` 2560x1440 (camera zoom 1.12, UI scale 1.0).

Opaque coverage counts pixels at alpha >= 0.5 on a paused, authored-scene-only render of the live cast (every CanvasItem outside the scene's subtree hidden, viewport transparent), sampled at stride 2. The arena-wide veil is a translucent tint (peak 0.28-0.34) that rises exactly once per cast and never reaches the opaque threshold, so it is excluded; `storm_longbow`'s veil is a single-shot full-viewport flash and declares coverage 1.0 at 0 Hz, the two darken veils declare 0.0. The reduced-motion device is the live cast's own camera shake read against the Player camera's baseline feet-lift offset: with `screen_shake` on the camera moves by up to 4.9-13.1 px (2K/648p runs) inside the shake window after the first impact, with it off the trace is exactly zero on every frame of every run, and the formation is identical at every beat (headless re-run in the gate).

## Observed readability at the beats

### `ranger/moon_crossbow`

Beats sampled (presentation clock, frame): release 0.88 s (frame 53), impact 1.2 s (frame 72), active 1.83 s (frame 110), recovery 2.85 s (frame 171); declared timing {"windup": 0.0, "release": 0.7, "active": 1.05, "recovery": 2.6, "cancel": 3.1}.

- **release** — The Ranger draws under the swelling night veil (0.21 alpha) with the aimed bolt streaking toward the nearest goblin; the three real enemies, their HP bars, the red hazard telegraph and the purple enemy bolt all stay readable.
- **impact** — 0.15 s after the active edge: the first split bolts land on the goblins with the weapon's impact bursts and the camera holds a 1.4-6 px shake offset in the normal frame (0 in the reduced-motion frame); damage numbers are already flying.
- **active** — The four split bolts fan around the mark over the enemy cluster (~60 px across at 648p, ~150 px at 2K); enemies, HUD and hazard are untouched by any opaque element.
- **recovery** — The bolts rejoin and fade; the veil eases to 0.20 and the frame is dominated by the world again.
- **crowded** — Twelve real goblins at the declared crowd cap around the aim point; the executor damages the targets it selects and the impact bursts land on them; HP bars and damage numbers make the read explicit; no enemy is occluded by an opaque element.
- **reduced motion** — Same cast with the shipped `screen_shake` toggle off: the timeline scene's shake device reads it and the Player camera stays at its baseline offset on every frame (trace max 0.0), so the world does not shift; the formation, hitstop and veil are identical.
- **photosensitivity-safe** — Toggle off and the one full-screen surface the live scene flags (`BackdropVeil`) zeroed after the cast begins; the frame differs from the reduced-motion frame by the missing tint only.

### `ranger/storm_longbow`

Beats sampled (presentation clock, frame): release 0.77 s (frame 46), impact 1.1 s (frame 66), active 1.9 s (frame 114), recovery 3.15 s (frame 189); declared timing {"windup": 0.0, "release": 0.6, "active": 0.95, "recovery": 2.85, "cancel": 3.45}.

- **release** — The corridor draws from the hero toward the enemies under the single-shot veil flash (0.28 peak at release); the corridor is legible as a lane at every viewport.
- **impact** — The corridor strikes the goblins with hit bursts and damage numbers while the camera shakes (up to 9 px); at 648p the small purple enemy bolt is swallowed by the light-blue flash for this beat (recorded as `projectile_readable: false` on 3 of 84 frames) — a genuine readability limitation of the flash, not a capture artefact.
- **active** — Seven lightning beats walk the corridor (~190 px at 648p, ~400 px at 2K); the enemies along the lane show their hit feedback.
- **recovery** — The corridor thins below the opaque threshold; the veil tail (0.05) is the only remaining presence.
- **crowded** — Twelve real goblins; the corridor runs through the crowd and the storm impact bursts and damage numbers land on the enemies inside it.
- **reduced motion** — Same cast, toggle off: camera trace 0.0 on every frame, formation identical, veil flash unchanged.
- **photosensitivity-safe** — Toggle off and the veil flash surface zeroed; the corridor and hits are unchanged and no full-screen surface is drawn.

### `ranger/hunter_trap`

Beats sampled (presentation clock, frame): release 1.17 s (frame 70), impact 1.55 s (frame 93), active 2.05 s (frame 123), recovery 3.25 s (frame 195); declared timing {"windup": 0.0, "release": 0.95, "active": 1.4, "recovery": 2.7, "cancel": 3.8}.

- **release** — Three trap rings close toward the centre under the forest veil (0.21); the rings are the largest Ranger silhouette and read clearly at every viewport.
- **impact** — The jaws snap on the enemy cluster: the trap-jaw impact flipbook plays on the nearest goblins and the camera takes the largest shake of the trio (up to 13 px) — the normal and reduced-motion impact frames differ by the whole world shifting while the formation is identical.
- **active** — The three-ring lock holds over the enemies; rune centres visible; the veil peaks at 0.34 and stays a tint.
- **recovery** — The rune bleeds out; the enemies and their HP bars remain fully readable through the fading veil.
- **crowded** — Twelve real goblins; the trap jaws close on the cluster and the flipbook plays on the nearest victims; the crowd stays readable above and below the formation.
- **reduced motion** — Same cast, toggle off: camera trace 0.0 on every frame, formation identical.
- **photosensitivity-safe** — Toggle off and the veil zeroed; the rings are unchanged.

## Limitations

- The world is a certification arena, not a spawned run: the shipped Player stands at the arena centre with its basic weapon held (its auto-attack VFX expects the game's Main scene as parent), the real Enemy scenes are frozen in place with 100000 HP so the composition depends only on the cast, and the arena floor is a flat bench colour rather than field art. Everything that moves or reads — Player, camera, enemies, hit feedback, damage numbers, HP bars, hazard telegraph, enemy projectile, HUD widget, executor, authored scene, victim impacts — is the shipped runtime.
- The shipped game has no dedicated photosensitivity setting; the photosensitivity-safe variant is `screen_shake` off plus capture-side zeroing of the surface the live scene flags as a full-screen layer, recorded per weapon as `fullscreen_suppressed` with the measured surface count.
- At 1152x648 the enemy bolt is a 17 px sprite; during storm_longbow's veil flash it drops under the contrast floor for one beat (3 of 84 frames), recorded as an observation rather than enforced.
- Ranger's executors outlive their presentations (4.45-5.35 s lifetimes against 3.1-3.8 s timelines), so no Ranger frame shows a released presentation; the hero's camera shake is the cast's only reduced-motion device and the combat director's hit shake is outside this harness.
