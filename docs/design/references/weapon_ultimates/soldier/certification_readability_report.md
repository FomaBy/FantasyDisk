# Soldier weapon-ultimate certification readability report (FAN-3941)

Independent-review evidence for the Soldier ultimate trio (`soldier/soldier_rifle`, `soldier/soldier_grenade`, `soldier/soldier_bayonet`) in the four presentation modes at the four supported viewports, taken from the real runtime: every frame is a real cast started through UltimatePlayerHost on the shipped Player against real Enemy scenes, a real hazard telegraph and a real enemy projectile, with the live ultimate HUD adapter, under a fixed 60 fps clock. For `soldier_grenade` the capture continues through the executor's 8.4 s: the chain detonation, the burning crater and the final fade are now drawn from the executor's own `.detonate` and `.crater` beats (FAN-3941 presentation-only instrumentation) and committed at every mode and viewport.

## Provenance

- Source ref/commit/tree the shipped runtime was run from: `dev` / `d192be10bbe52dd89971cab0acc66eb92ccab37f` / `e4a423855ffab4e8c83a2e5255fef4e65f4cf5cf`. Integrated origin/dev revision whose shipped Soldier scenes, executors, Player, Enemy, hazard and HUD were run; the capture tooling, frames and this manifest are added by FAN-3941 on top of it. The Soldier grenade executor and scene driver carry FAN-3941's presentation-only payoff instrumentation.
- Engine: Godot 4.7-stable (official) (gl_compatibility, opengl3, Apple M4 Pro), macOS 26.5.1, display server macOS, captured 2026-09-09 07:31:26 UTC, fixed frame clock 60 fps.
- Renderer: `tests/ultimates/presentation/soldier_certification_live_capture.gd`; gate: `tests/ultimates/presentation/soldier_certification_capture_test.gd`; capture manifest: `docs/design/references/weapon_ultimates/soldier/certification_capture_manifest.json` (every frame's path, size, byte count, sha256, camera state, world positions, probes and measurements; every run's camera-offset trace).
- Capture command: `python3 tools/godot_gate.py --path . --fixed-fps 60 --disable-vsync --script res://tests/ultimates/presentation/soldier_certification_live_capture.gd`
- Gate command: `python3 tools/godot_gate.py --headless --path . --fixed-fps 60 --script res://tests/ultimates/presentation/soldier_certification_capture_test.gd`
- Seed: 3941 (seed() is reset to this value before every cast; the shake device draws randf_range for its offsets, so the seed plus the fixed frame clock make every offset and every frame reproducible, and runs that differ only in a device stay pixel-comparable.)
- Exclusive process admission during the committed run: no. Exclusive admission is not used: the engine runs on a fixed 60 fps clock, so host load cannot change a pixel; the run honours the shared slot admission of `tools/godot_gate.py`.
- Real runtime: Player `res://scenes/Player.tscn`, enemies `res://scenes/Enemy.tscn`, hazards scripts/hazard_vfx.gd telegraph(radius 96) at Player offset (-190, 140) and scenes/EnemyProjectile.tscn at Player offset (-120, -180), HUD scripts/ui/ultimate_hud/ultimate_hud_runtime_adapter.gd mount() on the Player (real UltimateHudWidget, live charge/active state) plus an HP readout bound to Player.health/max_health; combat camera zoom 1.12 on the logical 2560x1440 canvas (canvas_items).
- Method: One real cast per weapon x mode x viewport in a SubViewport of the exact viewport size: the shipped Player (configure_character, full ultimate charge, its own Camera2D made current at combat zoom 1.12 x viewport_height/1440, smoothing off) at the origin; real Enemy scenes on a deterministic spiral around the aim centre (frozen in place, real HP, real hit feedback); a real HazardVfx telegraph and a real EnemyProjectile; the live UltimateHudRuntimeAdapter mounting the real UltimateHudWidget plus an HP readout bound to the Player; the cast started with UltimatePlayerHost.activate so the executor, authored scene, victim impacts and weight devices run as in the game. The engine runs under --fixed-fps 60: beat = frame index. Each frame samples the Player camera offset (reduced-motion evidence). The hero's basic weapon is held (its _process is off) so only the ultimate acts. At a beat the composite frame is read, then the tree is paused, every CanvasItem outside the authored scene's subtree is hidden and the viewport rendered transparent for the authored-scene opaque-coverage measurement (alpha >= 0.5, stride 2; bounding box against the HUD band), then restored. reduced_motion sets the shipped screen_shake toggle off on the tree root; photosensitivity_safe additionally zeroes the modulate of every node the live scene flags as fullscreen_layer; crowded stands the declared crowd cap of enemies. Readability probes project the recorded world positions through the recorded camera state.

## Observed readability at the beats

### `soldier/soldier_rifle`

Beats sampled (presentation clock, frame): release 0.75 s (frame 45), impact 1.05 s (frame 63), active 1.75 s (frame 105), recovery 2.9 s (frame 174); declared timing {"windup": 0.0, "release": 0.6, "active": 0.9, "recovery": 2.6, "cancel": 3.2}.

- **release** — The war banner is up and the spectral rifles have swung to aim; the first volley lane fades in over the goblins.
- **impact** — 1.05 s: the first volley lane is lit (0.55 alpha) with the aimed rank under the banner; the enemies in the lane show their hit feedback and damage numbers; the camera trace is zero (no shake device).
- **active** — 1.75 s: between the second and third volleys (flashes at 0.8, 1.9, 3.0 s), the lane and rank hold without a muzzle flash — the authored cadence, not a defect.
- **recovery** — 2.90 s: the rank holds its aim with the lane still lit; the smoke recall keyed at 4.6 s is outside the 3.2 s cancel.
- **crowded** — Twenty-two real goblins at the declared crowd cap; the executor's volleys hit the targets it selects, with the rifle impact flipbook and damage numbers on them.
- **reduced motion** — Same cast with `screen_shake` off: no device in the cast reads the toggle, the camera trace is 0.0 in both runs, identical frame (intrinsic no-op).
- **photosensitivity-safe** — Toggle off; zero full-screen surfaces measured on the live scene, so nothing to suppress; frame equals the reduced-motion frame (intrinsic no-op).

### `soldier/soldier_grenade`

Beats sampled (presentation clock, frame): release 0.85 s (frame 51), impact 1.15 s (frame 69), active 1.95 s (frame 117), recovery 3.2 s (frame 192), detonation 5.6 s (frame 336), crater 7.15 s (frame 429), final 8.2 s (frame 492); declared timing {"windup": 0.0, "release": 0.7, "active": 1.0, "recovery": 2.9, "cancel": 3.5}.

- **release** — 0.85 s: the lob arc fades and the seven decorative grenade props sit around the Soldier while the executor has seeded its seven real grenades around the aim point.
- **impact** — 1.15 s: the fuse ring is at full size and the props hold; the enemies and their HP bars stay readable.
- **active** — 1.95 s: the fuse ring pulses (1.14 Hz) and shrinks; no detonation yet — the executor's first impact is at 4.7 s.
- **recovery** — 3.20 s: the authored 3.5 s timeline is ending with the ring small and the props still seeded; the presentation stays live because the executor runs to 8.4 s.
- **detonation** — 5.60 s: the fourth blast of the outside-in chain: the orange ChainBlast ring pulses at the executor's real blast point among the enemies, the blasted props have burned away one per detonation, victims carry the grenade impact flipbook and their damage numbers; six more blasts follow every 0.3 s at different points, so no region flashes repeatedly.
- **crater** — 7.15 s: the FireColumn stands on the crater centre (the executor's aim target) with the ring marking the 190-unit crater radius; the goblins inside it show the crater tick damage (48).
- **final** — 8.20 s: the column and ring are fading out ahead of the executor's 8.4 s completion, which releases the presentation.
- **crowded** — Twenty-six real goblins at the declared crowd cap; every target takes its deduplicated chain hit and the crater ticks inside the radius, the grenade impact flipbook rides the shared ripple (pool cap 24), and the detonation/crater/final frames are committed at every viewport.
- **reduced motion** — Same cast, toggle off: zero camera trace in both runs, identical frames at every beat including the payoff beats (intrinsic no-op).
- **photosensitivity-safe** — Toggle off; zero full-screen surfaces despite the declared `flash` presence backdrop; frame equals the reduced-motion frame (intrinsic no-op); the chain blasts are ring-sized and each lands at a different point.

## Limitations

- The Soldier class manifest declares `presence` devices (darken/flash backdrop, camera shake, hitstop, time-scale dip) and a raised-banner cast pose that the shipped scenes and runtime do not realise; both accessibility variants are therefore measured intrinsic no-ops. `soldier_grenade` declares a `flash` backdrop that no surface renders, so its quality block declares the flash that exists (none). Recorded for a PM decision, not repaired.
- The grenade payoff is drawn from real executor events with existing nodes only: the chain-blast ring pulses 0.28 s per detonation on one node (the 0.3 s chain interval keeps blasts from overlapping), the seeded props burn away one per detonation in chain order, and the fire column rises on the crater centre and fades after the last tick; the executor's 8.4 s completion releases the presentation, so the final fade is cut at 8.4 s (the `final` beat at 8.2 s shows it fading).
- The rifle's mid-active beat lands between muzzle-flash volleys (authored cadence); the smoke recall (4.6 s) and the bayonet rally salute (3.5-3.9 s) keys outside the cancel never play in the authored timeline, though the executors outlive it (5.6 s / 4.25 s).
- The world is a certification arena, not a spawned run: the shipped Player stands at the arena centre with its basic weapon held, the real Enemy scenes are frozen in place with 100000 HP, and the floor is a flat bench colour; Player, camera, enemies, hit feedback, damage numbers, HP bars, hazard, projectile, HUD, executor, authored scene and victim impacts are the shipped runtime.
- The shipped game has no dedicated photosensitivity setting; for Soldier the photosensitivity-safe variant is `screen_shake` off with nothing to suppress (measured), recorded per weapon.
