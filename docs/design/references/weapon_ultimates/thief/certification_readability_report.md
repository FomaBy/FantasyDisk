# Thief weapon-ultimate certification readability report (FAN-3941)

Independent-review evidence for the Thief ultimate trio (`thief/thief_coin_pouch`, `thief/thief_shadow_cloak`, `thief/thief_smoke_bomb`) in the four presentation modes at the four supported viewports, regenerated after the PM-granted lifetime repair: a naturally completed cast now keeps its authored presentation until its declared cancel, so every Thief beat is live in the real game. Every frame is a real cast started through UltimatePlayerHost on the shipped Player (its own current Camera2D, real HP, a full ultimate charge), against real Enemy scenes, a real hazard telegraph and a real enemy projectile, with the live ultimate HUD adapter, under a fixed 60 fps clock and the exclusive process admission of `tools/godot_gate.py`; the reduced-motion and photosensitivity-safe modes are the persisted production preferences published on the tree root through `scripts/settings/ultimate_accessibility_settings.gd` and honoured by the class scene driver itself. The frames are the evidence; the capture manifest pins every file hash, the source commit and tree read from git at capture time, the engine, the commands, the per-frame camera/world state, the accessibility snapshot, the measured coverage, the full-screen surface alpha and the per-run camera and surface traces.

- Engine: Godot 4.7-stable (official) (gl_compatibility, opengl3, Apple M4 Pro), macOS 26.5.1, display server macOS, captured 2026-09-09 14:49:24 UTC, fixed frame clock 60 fps.

- Capture command: `FSD_GODOT_EXCLUSIVE=1 python3 tools/godot_gate.py --path . --fixed-fps 60 --disable-vsync --script res://tests/ultimates/presentation/thief_certification_live_capture.gd`
- Gate command: `python3 tools/godot_gate.py --headless --path . --fixed-fps 60 --script res://tests/ultimates/presentation/thief_certification_capture_test.gd`
- Seed: 3941 (seed() is reset to this value before every cast; the shake device draws randf_range for its offsets, so the seed plus the fixed frame clock make every offset and every frame reproducible, and runs that differ only in a device stay pixel-comparable.)
- Exclusive process admission during the committed run: yes. Every certification cast ran under the explicit exclusive process admission of `tools/godot_gate.py` (`FSD_GODOT_EXCLUSIVE=1`, recorded as `capture.exclusive_gate: true`) on the fixed 60 fps clock; the renderer refuses to run without it, and refuses a dirty worktree, so the recorded source commit is the exact source the frames were rendered from.
- Real runtime: Player `res://scenes/Player.tscn`, enemies `res://scenes/Enemy.tscn`, hazards scripts/hazard_vfx.gd telegraph(radius 96) at Player offset (-190, 140) and scenes/EnemyProjectile.tscn at Player offset (-120, -180), HUD scripts/ui/ultimate_hud/ultimate_hud_runtime_adapter.gd mount() on the Player (real UltimateHudWidget, live charge/active state) plus an HP readout bound to Player.health/max_health; combat camera zoom 1.12 on the logical 2560x1440 canvas (canvas_items).
- Method: One real cast per weapon x mode x viewport in a SubViewport of the exact viewport size: the shipped Player (configure_character, full ultimate charge, its own Camera2D made current at combat zoom 1.12 x viewport_height/1440, smoothing off) at the origin; real Enemy scenes on a deterministic spiral around the aim centre (frozen in place, real HP, real hit feedback); a real HazardVfx telegraph and a real EnemyProjectile; the live UltimateHudRuntimeAdapter mounting the real UltimateHudWidget plus an HP readout bound to the Player; the cast started with UltimatePlayerHost.activate so the executor, authored scene, victim impacts and weight devices run as in the game. The engine runs under --fixed-fps 60: beat = frame index. Each frame samples the Player camera offset (reduced-motion evidence). The hero's basic weapon is held (its _process is off) so only the ultimate acts. At a beat the composite frame is read and the tree is paused; every CanvasItem outside the authored scene's subtree is hidden and the viewport rendered transparent for the authored-scene opaque-coverage measurement (alpha >= 0.5, stride 2; bounding box against the HUD band), then restored, the caption drawn and the committed frame read while still paused, so the PNG, the camera record and the coverage describe the same process step; the tree resumes afterwards. reduced_motion and photosensitivity_safe publish the persisted ultimate_accessibility_settings snapshot on the tree root (scripts/settings/ultimate_accessibility_settings.gd apply_snapshot, as main.gd does at startup) and the live cast's own driver honours it; the capture runner never touches the scene. The full-screen surface alpha of the live scene is sampled every frame. crowded stands the declared crowd cap of enemies. Readability probes project the recorded world positions through the recorded camera state.

## Observed readability at the beats

### `thief/thief_coin_pouch`

Beats sampled (presentation clock, frame): release 0.9 s (frame 54), impact 1.25 s (frame 75), active 1.7 s (frame 102), recovery 2.68 s (frame 161); declared timing {"windup": 0.0, "release": 0.7, "active": 1.1, "recovery": 2.3, "cancel": 3.05}.

- **release** — 0.90 s: the thirteen-coin fan opens over the aim centre under the darken veil (0.25) with the hero pose lit; the executor has already hopped its coins to the three targets (0.32 s) and their pouch flipbooks and damage numbers are fading; hazard rim 0.30, bolt 0.18, enemies 0.29-0.34.
- **impact** — 1.25 s: the first-impact hitstop (100 ms) and the camera shake (offset 0.6, -1.3 px at 648p, up to 6.5 px in the window) under the full veil (0.30); the coin formation is mid-ricochet; Player 0.55.
- **active** — 1.70 s: the crown-burst return under the full veil (0.30); coverage 0.0031; enemies 0.29-0.34; gameplay inactive since 0.32 s.
- **recovery** — 2.68 s: the coins settle and the veil eases (0.16); coverage 0.0042; the enemies' feedback remains readable.
- **crowded** — Fifteen real goblins at the declared crowd cap: the executor hops a coin to each target (1.32 s), the pouch flipbook plays on every victim and the damage numbers show the hits; the first impact fires the hitstop, the 6.5 px shake and the 0.30 veil, and the presentation drains to 3.05 s.
- **reduced motion** — Same cast with `ultimate_reduced_motion: true` published on the tree root through `scripts/settings/ultimate_accessibility_settings.gd` (the production preference, exactly as main.gd publishes the saved settings; the shipped `screen_shake` toggle stays on): the scene driver's own shake device reads the preference and leaves the Player camera offset at 0.0 on every frame (per-run camera trace in the capture manifest; the normal twin's trace shows the first-impact shake), the veil, the hero pose, the hitstop and every formation beat stay on the unchanged timing.
- **photosensitivity-safe** — Same cast with `ultimate_photosensitivity_safe: true` published through `scripts/settings/ultimate_accessibility_settings.gd`: the scene driver never lights its arena-wide BackdropVeil (the only full-screen surface the cast draws; per-run `fullscreen_alpha_peak` 0.0 against the lit normal twin), the camera device, the hitstop and the formation are unchanged; the capture runner touches nothing in the scene.

## Limitations

- The world is a certification arena, not a spawned run: the shipped Player stands at the arena centre with its basic weapon held, the real Enemy scenes are frozen in place with 100000 HP, and the arena floor is a flat bench colour rather than field art. Everything that moves or reads is the shipped runtime.
- The coin-pouch and shadow-cloak executors still complete early (0.32 s / 1.27 s with three targets); gameplay ends there, exactly as before. The authored presentation now drains to its declared cancel through the PM-granted runtime change (`presentation_natural_completion_lifetime_test.gd` proves both keys reach active and recovery with gameplay inactive, and that cancel, death, reset, exit and a replacing activation still release at once). 84 of the 84 Thief frames show a live presentation.
- The shadow-cloak Player read sits at 0.13-0.15 through the cast (floor 0.12): the dark cloak silhouette over the dark floor is the weapon's identity, and the hero pose and the veil rim keep the hero locatable.
- The enemy bolt is a 17 px sprite at 1152x648 with a native contrast share of 0.14-0.18; no Thief frame takes it below 0.14.
