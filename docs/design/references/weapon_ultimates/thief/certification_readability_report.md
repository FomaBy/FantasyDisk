# Thief weapon-ultimate certification readability report (FAN-3941)

Independent-review evidence for the Thief ultimate trio (`thief/thief_coin_pouch`, `thief/thief_shadow_cloak`, `thief/thief_smoke_bomb`) in the four presentation modes at the four supported viewports, taken from the real runtime: every frame is a real cast started through UltimatePlayerHost on the shipped Player against real Enemy scenes, a real hazard telegraph and a real enemy projectile, with the live ultimate HUD adapter, under a fixed 60 fps clock. This card also normalises the Thief class manifest (per-weapon direction fields, frozen `phase_ids`, `generator_provenance`, `evidence.capture_script`).

## Provenance

- Source ref/commit/tree the shipped runtime was run from: `dev` / `d192be10bbe52dd89971cab0acc66eb92ccab37f` / `e4a423855ffab4e8c83a2e5255fef4e65f4cf5cf`. Integrated origin/dev revision whose shipped Thief scenes, executors, Player, Enemy, hazard and HUD were run; the capture tooling, frames and this manifest are added by FAN-3941 on top of it. The Soldier grenade executor and scene driver carry FAN-3941's presentation-only payoff instrumentation.
- Engine: Godot 4.7-stable (official) (gl_compatibility, opengl3, Apple M4 Pro), macOS 26.5.1, display server macOS, captured 2026-09-09 08:44:06 UTC, fixed frame clock 60 fps.
- Renderer: `tests/ultimates/presentation/thief_certification_live_capture.gd`; gate: `tests/ultimates/presentation/thief_certification_capture_test.gd`; capture manifest: `docs/design/references/weapon_ultimates/thief/certification_capture_manifest.json` (every frame's path, size, byte count, sha256, camera state, world positions, probes and measurements; every run's camera-offset trace).
- Capture command: `python3 tools/godot_gate.py --path . --fixed-fps 60 --disable-vsync --script res://tests/ultimates/presentation/thief_certification_live_capture.gd`
- Gate command: `python3 tools/godot_gate.py --headless --path . --fixed-fps 60 --script res://tests/ultimates/presentation/thief_certification_capture_test.gd`
- Seed: 3941 (seed() is reset to this value before every cast; the shake device draws randf_range for its offsets, so the seed plus the fixed frame clock make every offset and every frame reproducible, and runs that differ only in a device stay pixel-comparable.)
- Exclusive process admission during the committed run: no. Exclusive admission is not used: the engine runs on a fixed 60 fps clock, so host load cannot change a pixel; the run honours the shared slot admission of `tools/godot_gate.py`.
- Real runtime: Player `res://scenes/Player.tscn`, enemies `res://scenes/Enemy.tscn`, hazards scripts/hazard_vfx.gd telegraph(radius 96) at Player offset (-190, 140) and scenes/EnemyProjectile.tscn at Player offset (-120, -180), HUD scripts/ui/ultimate_hud/ultimate_hud_runtime_adapter.gd mount() on the Player (real UltimateHudWidget, live charge/active state) plus an HP readout bound to Player.health/max_health; combat camera zoom 1.12 on the logical 2560x1440 canvas (canvas_items).
- Method: One real cast per weapon x mode x viewport in a SubViewport of the exact viewport size: the shipped Player (configure_character, full ultimate charge, its own Camera2D made current at combat zoom 1.12 x viewport_height/1440, smoothing off) at the origin; real Enemy scenes on a deterministic spiral around the aim centre (frozen in place, real HP, real hit feedback); a real HazardVfx telegraph and a real EnemyProjectile; the live UltimateHudRuntimeAdapter mounting the real UltimateHudWidget plus an HP readout bound to the Player; the cast started with UltimatePlayerHost.activate so the executor, authored scene, victim impacts and weight devices run as in the game. The engine runs under --fixed-fps 60: beat = frame index. Each frame samples the Player camera offset (reduced-motion evidence). The hero's basic weapon is held (its _process is off) so only the ultimate acts. At a beat the composite frame is read, then the tree is paused, every CanvasItem outside the authored scene's subtree is hidden and the viewport rendered transparent for the authored-scene opaque-coverage measurement (alpha >= 0.5, stride 2; bounding box against the HUD band), then restored. reduced_motion sets the shipped screen_shake toggle off on the tree root; photosensitivity_safe additionally zeroes the modulate of every node the live scene flags as fullscreen_layer; crowded stands the declared crowd cap of enemies. Readability probes project the recorded world positions through the recorded camera state.

## Observed readability at the beats

### `thief/thief_coin_pouch`

Beats sampled (presentation clock, frame): release 0.9 s (frame 54), impact 1.25 s (frame 75), active 1.7 s (frame 102), recovery 2.68 s (frame 161); declared timing {"windup": 0.0, "release": 0.7, "active": 1.1, "recovery": 2.3, "cancel": 3.05}.

- **release** — 0.90 s: with three targets the executor has already completed (0.32 s) and the host has released the presentation — the frame shows the Thief, the enemies with their coin-pouch impact bursts fading and damage numbers, no timeline element (`presentation_live: false`). At the crowd cap (13 targets, 1.28 s) the same beat still shows the coin formation.
- **impact** — 1.25 s: released with three targets; live at the crowd cap, where thirteen coins hop between the goblins with the pouch flipbook on each victim.
- **active** — 1.70 s: released in every mode — the declared active window never plays in the real game.
- **recovery** — 2.68 s: released in every mode.
- **crowded** — Thirteen real goblins at the declared crowd cap: the executor hops a coin to each target (1.28 s), the pouch flipbook plays on every victim and the damage numbers show the hits; this is the only mode in which the coin timeline is live at the impact beat.
- **reduced motion** — Same cast with `screen_shake` off: no device in the cast reads the toggle, the camera trace is 0.0 with the toggle on and off, and the frame is the normal frame by construction (recorded as an intrinsic no-op).
- **photosensitivity-safe** — Toggle off; the live scene flags no full-screen layer (measured 0), so there is nothing to suppress and the frame equals the reduced-motion frame (intrinsic no-op).

### `thief/thief_shadow_cloak`

Beats sampled (presentation clock, frame): release 1.02 s (frame 61), impact 1.37 s (frame 82), active 1.9 s (frame 114), recovery 2.89 s (frame 173); declared timing {"windup": 0.0, "release": 0.82, "active": 1.22, "recovery": 2.57, "cancel": 3.2}.

- **release** — 1.02 s: the eight marks and the first phantom stabs are live over the goblins (the executor completes at 1.27 s); violet plate small at 648p, readable at 1080p and 2K.
- **impact** — 1.37 s: released in every mode (executor completed at 1.27 s).
- **active** — 1.90 s: released in every mode — the declared active window never plays in the real game.
- **recovery** — 2.89 s: released in every mode.
- **crowded** — Thirteen real goblins; the phantom stabs and their impact bursts land on the nearest victims before the 1.27 s completion.
- **reduced motion** — Same cast, toggle off: zero camera trace in both runs, identical frame, intrinsic no-op.

### `thief/thief_smoke_bomb`

Beats sampled (presentation clock, frame): release 1.14 s (frame 68), impact 1.49 s (frame 89), active 2.09 s (frame 125), recovery 3.13 s (frame 188); declared timing {"windup": 0.0, "release": 0.94, "active": 1.34, "recovery": 2.84, "cancel": 3.42}.

- **release** — 1.14 s: the dome expands around the Thief (~110 px at 648p) with the pressure marks drifting outward; the goblins and their HP bars stay readable through the translucent smoke.

- **crowded** — Thirteen real goblins; the dome and the smoke impact bursts sit among the crowd, which stays readable through the translucent smoke.
- **reduced motion** — Same cast, toggle off: zero camera trace in both runs, identical frame, intrinsic no-op.
- **photosensitivity-safe** — Toggle off; zero full-screen surfaces; frame equals the reduced-motion frame (intrinsic no-op).

## Limitations

- Runtime finding for the PM: the Thief coin pouch and shadow cloak executors complete in 0.32-1.28 s and 1.27 s respectively, and UltimatePlayerHost releases the authored presentation when the activation completes, so their declared 3.05 s / 3.20 s timelines are cut at that point in the real game; 50 of the 84 committed frames show a released presentation and are recorded as such (`presentation_live`, `presentation_released_seconds`). Executors are immutable on this card and the phase envelope is contract-owned, so nothing was changed; the class manifests' timing declarations remain honest about the authored timeline, and the capture manifest records when the runtime actually releases it.
- The Thief class manifest declares `presence` devices (darken backdrop, camera shake, hitstop, time dip) that the shipped timeline scenes and runtime do not realise; the accessibility variants are therefore measured intrinsic no-ops. Recorded for a PM decision, not repaired.
- The world is a certification arena, not a spawned run: the shipped Player stands at the arena centre with its basic weapon held, the real Enemy scenes are frozen in place with 100000 HP, and the floor is a flat bench colour; Player, camera, enemies, hit feedback, damage numbers, HP bars, hazard, projectile, HUD, executor, authored scene and victim impacts are the shipped runtime.
- The shipped game has no dedicated photosensitivity setting; for Thief the photosensitivity-safe variant is `screen_shake` off with nothing to suppress (measured), recorded per weapon.
